## 2. Why this doesn't match the naive per-month sum — and by how much

Summing `monthly_lpg_insights.csv`'s own `difference` column for these 63 months gives **R190,599.43** — R50,129.94 more than the raw bridge above. Same structural reason already established for the whole-history version of this question: the per-month view only ever records what a month's *own* billing needed, capped at that amount. Any payment whose real gross exceeded what its assigned month needed just doesn't show up anywhere in the per-month columns — see §3.

