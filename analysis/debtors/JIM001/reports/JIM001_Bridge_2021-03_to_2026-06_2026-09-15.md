## 2. The two exclusion rules, as applied

### 2.1 Rule 1 — invoice `00033830` is not counted (it was credited back)

**Status: PROVEN.** Invoice `00033830` (2024-07-04) is three `INT-30` interest lines totalling **R1,051.83** — R384.87 + R315.87 + R351.09. Credit note `00012313`, dated **the same day**, reverses all three line-for-line: −R384.87, −R315.87, −R351.09.

It is nevertheless **allocated by receipt `34425`**, which the ERP's *View Payment Allocations* screen confirms directly. So R1,051.83 of that receipt's cash was consumed by an invoice that no longer exists. Under rule 1 that cash is removed from the LPG-available side, which raises the gap by exactly R1,051.83.

**This is the only place rule 1 bites.** Across all five ERP allocation screens — receipts `33810`, `34425`, `35270`, `36139`, `36988` — invoice `33830` is the **only** allocated invoice carrying any non-LPG value. Every other allocated invoice is pure LPG.

### 3.5 ⚠️ New finding — doc `35270` is double-counted, R18,441.12

**Status: the error is PROVEN; the proposed fix is Tier 3 / ASSERTED.**

`monthly_lpg_insights.csv` credits doc `35270` (R18,441.12, banked 2024-12-04) **twice**:

- to **2023-12**, for its full gross R18,441.12, leaving a R293.34 shortfall; and
- to **2024-05 and 2024-08**, where it is `ERP-PROVEN` against invoices 34849, 35094, 35297, 35522, 35749.

The ERP's *View Payment Allocations* screen for `35270` is decisive: it allocates to invoices **32531, 32614, 34849, 35094, 35297, 35522, 35749** — every one a 2024 invoice, **none from December 2023**. The 2024 attribution is observed; the 2023-12 attribution is inferred and wrong. A receipt banked 2024-12-04 was never going to be December 2023's payer.

**So 2023-12 has no identified payer, and its true shortfall is R18,734.46 — not R293.34.**

**Where its money probably is.** The three unapplied 2023/2024 docs in §3.4 sum to within **six cents** of 2023-12's billing:

```
27468  R13,528.54
28893  R 2,583.36
30269  R 2,622.50
──────────────────
       R18,734.40    vs 2023-12 net LPG invoiced R18,734.46   (Δ R0.06)
```

**Calibration, so this isn't over-read:** of the 63 possible subsets of the six-doc unapplied pool, **exactly one** lands within R1.00 of the target, and it lands within R0.06. That is a strong exact-sum signal rather than combinatorial noise. It also survives removing doc `17578` from the pool, which is already spoken for.

**It is still not proof.** This account has now produced three separate readings that inferred allocation from aggregates and were all wrong (Bridge review §5.1–§5.4). The fix is cheap and known: **pull the ERP *View Payment Allocations* screens for receipts `27468`, `28893` and `30269`.** That is the same move that settled April–November 2024 outright, and 2023-09 → 2023-12 carries the same fingerprint — long payment lags and a chain of leftovers that mis-sequence against their assigned months.

Until then the bridge carries 2023-12 as the CSV records it, and shows the R18,441.12 as an explicit correcting line in §4 rather than silently restating it.
