## 1. Raw file format — confirmed, and a real inconsistency between the two years

Both files are quoted-CSV with an 11-column layout:

```
"LINE","PERIOD","DOCNO","ENTRY","DATE","INVNO","CUSTOMER/BANK REF","ORDER","REFERENCE","AMOUNT","BALANCE"
```

- **2023 file** (`TWK0022023.TXT`, sorted by DOCUMENT DATE): `INVNO` is populated for every `Invoice` row (= its own `DOCNO`) and every `Crd Note` row (= the doc it credits). This is the join key the task's Method §1 assumed, and it works cleanly for 2023 — confirmed by cross-checking that **zero** `Crd Note` rows in either file have an `INVNO` that fails to match some 2023/2024 invoice `DOCNO`.
- **2024 file** (`TWK0022024.TXT`, sorted by ENTRY TYPE) — **`INVNO` is blank on every single row, including every Invoice and every Crd Note.** This defeats the task's assumed join key entirely for 2024. There is no ERP-native field linking a 2024 CN to the invoice it settles.

**What I did instead:** matched 2024 `Crd Note` rows to `Invoice` rows by **exact opposite-amount matching** (preferring a shared `CUSTOMER/BANK REF` text where more than one candidate ties), since `CUSTOMER/BANK REF` — unlike `INVNO` — is populated on every row but is **not** a safe join key by itself: two values are reused across multiple rows and create real ambiguity that a naive text-only join gets wrong:

- `"D/N 9760"` is shared by invoice `00033800` (R8,724.99, the "product" line) and invoice `00034285` (R20,930.00, a same-day duplicate/empties line); only one CN (`00009393`, −R20,930.00) exists for that text. **My first pass matched this text-only and got it wrong** — it attached the CN to whichever invoice it iterated to first, leaving the other with a spurious non-zero (or spurious zero) net. Re-running with exact-amount-first matching fixes it: the CN reverses `00034285` (exact zero net) and `00033800` correctly carries its own R8,724.99, unreversed by any CN (it settles via remittance instead — confirmed in §2).
- `"EMPTY 10168"` is shared by invoice `00036927` (R19,320.00) and **two** CNs: `00010617` (−R17,100.00) and `00010723` (−R19,320.00). Only the second is an exact-amount match to the invoice; the first (−R17,100.00) does not correspond to any invoice gross amount anywhere in either file. It is not orphaned, though — it appears by name in `proforma_journals_2024.csv` (`BATCH-2024-11-26`, ref_discount −427.50) and `remittance_lines_2024.csv` (`on_remittance=true`), i.e. it is a remittance-confirmed CN that simply doesn't net to a specific invoice's exact gross — normal for a bulk-empties-return credit that doesn't line up 1:1 with a single delivery.

**Conclusion on parsing:** the format did not defeat parsing, but it took two passes to get right, and it's worth recording why: the 2024 file's blank `INVNO` column is a genuine data-quality gap relative to 2023 that forces a weaker join key (free-text reference), and that key collides for at least two document pairs. Any tool — including a future automated rebuild, and including my own first attempt — that joins 2024 CNs to invoices by `CUSTOMER/BANK REF` text alone, without also requiring the amounts to be exact opposites, will silently mis-tag these two pairs. I did not find evidence the *existing* recreated-ledger build made this mistake — its journal/batch references (`proforma_journals_2024.csv`) show correct doc-level linkage sourced from the remittance PDFs directly, not from a text-join over the raw TXT — but it's a live tripwire for any future rebuild that works from the raw file instead (see §6).

## 2. Every invoice, netted, and where the non-zero ones went

For every invoice in both years I checked (a) whether an in-file CN nets it to ~R0, **and independently** (b) whether the invoice itself appears as a paid `Invoice` line on any of `remittance_lines_2023.csv` / `_2024.csv` / `_2025.csv`. Doing both checks, rather than treating a zero net as automatically "resolved," matters: several invoices net to zero *and* separately appear on a remittance line as paid in full, with their offsetting CN appearing on the **same remittance batch as its own line** — meaning the "zero" is not evidence the invoice was cancelled, it's simply what you get when the invoice's gross value and its same-batch credit note happen to be equal. That is a materially different (and equally clean) story from a true cancellation, so I report both cases separately below.

The other six are fully evidenced twice over: paid on remittance *and* separately credited on remittance, both ties exact.

The other 39 invoices carry a non-zero `INVNO`-tag net purely because most invoices in this ERP are settled by **lump-sum STAT payments** that carry a blank `INVNO` (payments never tag a specific invoice in this export) — CN-tagging alone was never going to zero these out, and that's expected, not a red flag by itself. Cross-checking all 39 against `remittance_lines_2023.csv` + `remittance_lines_2024.csv` (for the two invoices settled via the early-2024 catch-up batch, `00023075` and `00024011` — see §3): **38 of 39 are on a remittance-advice line as a fully-priced `Invoice` entry**, confirming cash was received against the full gross amount (net of the standard 2.5% discount where eligible, or R0 discount for the registered late-payment exceptions). The one exception:

- `00026553` (14/11/2023, D/N 6859, R7,532.25) is not on any remittance line, but is credited by CN `00006816` for −R7,532.33 the same day — net **−R0.08**. This is an eight-cent quantity/rounding wash, not a payment gap.

### 2024 (30 invoices) — 6 net to ~R0 via exact-amount CN matching, 24 non-zero

Of the 6 zero-net invoices, 4 are (like the 2023 pattern) independently confirmed paid-in-full on remittance with their offsetting CN also separately listed (`00030763`, `00033801`, `00034343`, `00035092` — all on `BATCH-2024-04-26` / `BATCH-2024-08-26` / `BATCH-2024-09-26` remittance lines). Only 2 are true no-remittance cancellations:

- `00034285` (04/07/2024, D/N 9760, R20,930.00) — reversed same-day by CN `00009393` (−R20,930.00), no remittance appearance.
- `00036927` (03/10/2024, EMPTY 10168, R19,320.00) — reversed a week later by CN `00010723` (−R19,320.00), no remittance appearance.

The other 24 non-zero-net invoices are **all** on a remittance-advice line — 20 in `remittance_lines_2024.csv`, and 4 (`00039683`, `00040081`, `00040459`, `00040950` — Jan/Feb 2025 invoices, the tail end of the file just before the Mar 2025 export cutoff) confirmed paid in `BATCH-2025-01-31` / `BATCH-2025-03-31` in `remittance_lines_2025.csv`. `allocation_edges.csv` carries explicit cash-allocation rows (`AL-0107`, `AL-0108`) tying STAT 110 cash to invoice `00039022` specifically, confirming this isn't just a doc-number coincidence. This is exactly the expected aging pattern for invoices raised in the last 30–60 days of the window — they roll into next year's remittance batch, not into an unpaid void.

**Net result: 0 of 77 invoices have a non-zero net with no remittance evidence and no exact/near-exact reversal.**

## 5. What this does and doesn't mean for the R38,791.27 B/F carry

- Every 2023–2024 invoice's gross amount is accounted for by cash confirmed on a remittance advice, or by an exact/near-zero credit-note reversal. There is no invoice sitting in the two years' raw exports whose full or partial amount has no evidence of ever being settled.
- The R38,791.27 that rolls forward as `BALANCE B/F` is therefore not concealing a specific named, still-open, still-collectible 2023–2024 invoice. It is (per the already-ratified bridge in `TWK002_Pre_Mar2025_BF_Bridge_2026-08-11.md`) the running-total residue of the batch-level payment/discount mechanics documented there — a claim this report's invoice-level check is consistent with, not merely repeats.
- This finding does **not** touch the separate, still-open H-027 challenge about whether the `phantom_cn_nets` (+R9,894.01) component of the *current-window* R8,084.67 residual represents real collectible debt. That dispute is about batch-level payment-vs-remittance gaps carried into 2025–2026 postings, not about any 2023–2024 invoice — this report finds no invoice-shaped candidate to add to that debate.

## Tripwires

- If a future rebuild of the 2023–2024 recreated ledgers assumes `INVNO`-based CN tagging works uniformly across both years, it will silently produce **zero** CN linkage for all of 2024 (§1) — check for this explicitly before trusting any such rebuild's per-invoice output.
- `CUSTOMER/BANK REF` text is not a safe unique join key on its own in 2024 (`"D/N 9760"` and `"EMPTY 10168"` each collide across two rows) — any future automated 2024 netting must pair on text **and** exact amount, or it will misattribute a CN to the wrong invoice.
- This report's remittance coverage stops at `remittance_lines_2025.csv` for the four tail invoices that spill into 2025; it does not re-verify the 2025+ remittance PDFs behind that CSV independently — if that transcription is ever found to be wrong, revisit §2's "24 non-zero, all confirmed" claim for 2024.
