## Q3 — twin re-pointing

**Can the lock mechanism express a swap?** Not as a "swap" operation. A lock names the exact documents a payment settles; the matcher then re-ties whatever is left. So a re-pointing is written as a lock on the payment with the customer-evidenced invoice set, and the displaced twin returns to the open pool. This works (PROVEN below). It cannot say "exchange A for B" in one statement, and two coupled swaps (T0092 / T0093) need two locks.
